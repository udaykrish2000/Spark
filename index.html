Spark Social Education & Welfare Society

Creating Pathways to Education, Opportunity and Independent Futures

SPARK Social Education and Welfare Society works with children and young people from underserved and rural communities to help them access education, develop essential skills, and build a more independent future.

Through our Higher Education Hostel & Mentoring Programme and Community Learning Centre – Anandghar, we provide educational support, safe spaces, mentoring, life skills and opportunities for personal growth

Founder

Mukund Kale

From receiving support to creating opportunities for others

Mukund Kale comes from a rural background in Maharashtra. His own educational journey shaped his understanding of how strongly a student's environment can influence their ability to learn and progress.

After completing his LLB, Mukund began supporting students from rural communities who faced limited financial resources, lack of accommodation and lack of guidance.

His personal experience of receiving support during his own higher education became the foundation for his commitment to helping other young people continue their educational journeys.

A belief that continues to guide SPARK:
“The right environment can change a student's journey.”

SPARK continues to build supportive environments where young people can learn, seek guidance, make informed choices and work towards independent futures.

SPARK's work is centred around two core programmes that create supportive pathways to education, confidence and independence.

Our Programme

    Our Story

    From One Student's Experience to an Organisation

    SPARK Social Education and Welfare Society was established in 2016 and is based in Pune, Maharashtra. The organisation's work is rooted in the belief that education becomes more accessible when young people have the right environment, support and guidance around them.

    Higher Education Hostel & Mentoring

    Supporting rural youth who move to Pune for higher education.

    Community Learning – Anandghar

    Supporting children from underserved communities through after-school learning and holistic development.

    Community-rooted work

    SPARK also conducts career guidance camps and medical camps based on community needs.

    Focused on opportunity

    The aim is to help young people continue education, make informed choices and build independent futures.

    Our Approach

    More than education. An environment to grow.

    At SPARK, education is not only about academic learning. Children and young people also need safety, guidance, opportunities and confidence to navigate the future.

    A safe space to learn

    We create supportive environments where students can study and develop without being limited by their circumstances.

    Someone to guide them

    Mentoring and regular guidance help young people understand their choices and plan their next steps.

    Opportunities to explore

    Exposure to education, careers and skill-development opportunities helps young people make informed decisions.

    Skills for the future

    Our programmes build communication, life skills, decision-making, resilience and personal development.

    Confidence to decide

    We help children and young people express themselves, take responsibility and work towards independent futures.

    Community partnership

    Our work is shaped with children, families, mentors and local communities rather than designed from a distance.

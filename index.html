Who we are

A spark can light an entire future.

Spark Social Education and Welfare Society works alongside underserved communities to give every child access to quality education, safe shelter, healthcare and the skills to build an independent life. What began as one classroom has grown into a network of programs trusted by families across the region.

Our Mission

To break the cycle of poverty by delivering education, shelter and skill-building to every child who needs it.

Our Vision

A future where every child, regardless of background, grows up educated, healthy and self-reliant.

Our Values

Compassion, transparency and community partnership guide every program we run.

What we do

Programs built around real needs

Six focused programs designed to support a child from the classroom to a career.

Education Support

Free tuition, learning materials and mentorship that keep children in school and on track.

Hostel Facilities

Safe, nurturing residential care for children who cannot study while living at home.

Career Guidance

One-on-one counselling and mentorship that helps older students plan a real career path.

Skill Development

Vocational training in tailoring, computers and trades that lead directly to employment.

Health Camps

Regular medical check-ups, nutrition support and hygiene awareness for children and families.

Community Activities

Local events and workshops that bring families together and strengthen the wider community.
